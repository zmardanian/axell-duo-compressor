Blind listening test · hardware vs plugin

Can you hear the hardware?

You will compare recordings of a real opto leveling amplifier (a Teletronix LA-2A) and a real FET limiter (a 1176LN) with the same audio processed by Axell Duo Compressor. Every pair is loudness-matched, so level gives nothing away. Use headphones or monitors in a quiet room. It takes about 15 minutes.
